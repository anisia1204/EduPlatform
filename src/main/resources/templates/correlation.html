EN → BAC Correlation

Students sitting the National Evaluation in year X are expected to sit the BAC in year X+4. Correlation is measured at county level (Pearson coefficient on 42 county averages).

Methodology: Since public EN and BAC datasets use different candidate identifiers, individual-level matching is not possible. This analysis follows the aggregate approach used by Buza & Tușa (2024): comparing county-level averages between EN cohorts and BAC cohorts 4 years later.
